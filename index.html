About
A few things I believe

Build tools that solve specific problems

Generic solutions rarely work well. I build tools around specific use cases — a grading assistant, a reflection coach, a workflow diagnostic. When a tool is purpose-built, people see the utility and embrace it.

Prototype quickly to learn what works

I build solutions every day and get things in front of real users early. If they provide practical utility they get refined and iterated upon. If not, I learn from the experience and archive them.

Privacy and local-first options matter

Not everything needs to live in the cloud. I build local-first tools that keep data on your hardware, giving you control and privacy without sacrificing functionality.

Educational tools should be hands-on

The best learning happens through discovery, not instruction. My vocational curriculum design uses gap/fill pedagogy — students earn access to tools by solving problems, not by following tutorials.
