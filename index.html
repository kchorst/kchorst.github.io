About

Hi, I'm Kevin.

I help small businesses in the Tri-Cities — attorneys, therapists, accountants, and other professionals — get more out of technology without getting lost in it. No jargon, no unnecessary complexity. Just practical solutions that actually work for your practice.

On the technical side, I build AI tools, browser extensions, and web applications. I'm particularly interested in making AI genuinely useful — not as a buzzword, but as something that saves you real time on real tasks.

I also design curriculum for vocational technology programs and have a deep interest in language systems and multilingual data. If that sounds eclectic, it is — and it means I bring an unusually broad perspective to whatever problem you're facing.
